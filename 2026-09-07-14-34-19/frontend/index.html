Dijkstra's Algorithm Internal Working & Mathematical Proof

Dijkstra computes exact ground-truth customer visit sequence permutations and shortest physical paths on dynamic SUMO graph G(t) using priority-queue node relaxation.

1. Mathematical Optimization Principle & Step-by-Step Equations

Step 1: Permutation Search Space Enumerable Set
$$\Pi(N) = \text{Permutations}(C_1, C_2, \dots, C_N), \quad |\Pi(N)| = N!$$
Step 2: Ground-Truth Optimal Sequence Objective Function
$$\pi^* = \arg\min_{\pi \in \Pi(N)} \left[ \sum_{k=1}^N d_{\text{path}}\left(C_{\pi(k-1)}, C_{\pi(k)}\right) \right], \quad \text{where } C_{\pi(0)} = O \text{ (Depot)}$$
Step 3: Leg Shortest Path via Priority-Queue Relaxation
$$d[v] = \min \left( d[v], d[u] + w_e(t) \right) \quad \forall v \in \text{Adj}(u)$$
Step 4: Dynamic SUMO Edge Weight Formula \(w_e(t)\)
$$w_e(t) = T_e(t) = \frac{L_e}{\max(v_e(t), 0.1)} \times \left( 1 + \text{penalty}_e \right)$$
Step 5: Accumulated Physical Route Metrics
$$T_{\text{total}} = \sum_{e \in \text{Path}} T_e(t), \quad D_{\text{total}} = \sum_{e \in \text{Path}} L_e, \quad v_{\text{avg}} = \frac{D_{\text{total}}}{\max(T_{\text{total}}, 0.1)}$$

QAOA Link-Based VRP Solver Internal Working & Mathematical Proof

Formulated strictly according to Azfar, Raisuddin, Ke, Holguín-Veras (ACM Trans. Quantum Comput., 2025 / arXiv:2505.01614).

1. Research Paper Mathematical Pipeline & Step-by-Step Formulations

Step 1: Decision Variable Formulation & Qubit Mapping
$$x_{i,p} \in \{0, 1\} \quad (\text{Customer } i \text{ visited at position } p), \quad n_{\text{qubits}} = N^2$$
Step 2: Position-Based QUBO Objective & Uniqueness Penalties
$$H_{\text{QUBO}} = \sum_{i=1}^N c_{0, i} x_{i, 1} + \sum_{p=1}^{N-1} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j \ne i}^N c_{i, j} x_{i, p} x_{j, p+1} + P \sum_{i=1}^N \left( \sum_{p=1}^N x_{i,p} - 1 \right)^2 + P \sum_{p=1}^N \left( \sum_{i=1}^N x_{i,p} - 1 \right)^2$$
Step 3: Penalty Multiplier Scaling (Azfar et al. Sec 4.5)
$$P = 2 \times \sum_{i,j} |c_{i,j}|, \quad Q_{\text{norm}} = \frac{Q}{\max_{i,j} |Q_{i,j}|}$$
Step 4: Ising Spin Transformation & Cost Hamiltonian
$$x_k \to \frac{Z_k + I}{2} \implies H_C = c_0 I + \sum_{k=1}^n h_k Z_k + \sum_{1 \le i < j \le n} J_{i,j} Z_i Z_j$$
Step 5: QAOA Variational Quantum Ansatz & X-Mixer Operator
$$|\psi(\boldsymbol{\gamma}, \boldsymbol{\beta})\rangle = \prod_{l=1}^p e^{-i \beta_l H_M} e^{-i \gamma_l H_C} |+\rangle^{\otimes n}, \quad H_M = \sum_{k=1}^n X_k$$
Step 6: Classical Optimization (COBYLA)
$$\min_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}, \boldsymbol{\beta}} E(\boldsymbol{\gamma}, \boldsymbol{\beta}) = \langle \psi(\boldsymbol{\gamma}, \boldsymbol{\beta}) | H_C | \psi(\boldsymbol{\gamma}, \boldsymbol{\beta}) \rangle$$

QPSO Swarm Router Internal Working & Mathematical Proof

Formulated strictly according to Herrera, Coelho, Steiner (Pesquisa Operacional 35(3), 2015).

1. Research Paper Mathematical Pipeline & Step-by-Step Formulations

Step 1: Continuous Swarm Representation & Search Dimension
$$\mathbf{x}_i = (x_{i,1}, x_{i,2}, \dots, x_{i,D}) \in \mathbb{R}^D, \quad D = N \text{ (Number of Customers)}$$
Step 2: Mean Best Position Vector (Herrera et al. Eq. 11)
$$\mathbf{mbest}_d = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{i=1}^M pbest_{i,d} \quad \forall d \in \{1, \dots, D\}$$
Step 3: Learning Inclination Point (LIP) \(p_{i,d}\)
$$p_{i,d} = \frac{\phi_1 \cdot pbest_{i,d} + \phi_2 \cdot gbest_d}{\phi_1 + \phi_2}, \quad \phi_1, \phi_2 \sim \text{Uniform}(0, 1)$$
Step 4: Quantum Wave Collapse Position Update (Eq. 13 & Algorithm 1)
$$x_{i,d}(t+1) = p_{i,d} \pm \alpha(t) \cdot |\mathbf{mbest}_d - x_{i,d}(t)| \cdot \ln\left(\frac{1}{u}\right), \quad u \sim \text{Uniform}(0, 1)$$
Step 5: Linear Contraction-Expansion Decay \(\alpha(t)\)
$$\alpha(t) = \alpha_{\text{start}} - \frac{t}{T_{\max} - 1} (\alpha_{\text{start}} - \alpha_{\text{end}}), \quad \alpha(0) = 1.0 \to \alpha(T_{\max}) = 0.5$$
Step 6: Rank Discretization Mapping (Sec 3.3, pp. 14-15)
$$\pi = \text{Argsort}(\mathbf{x}_i) \implies \text{Visit Sequence } = [O, C_{\pi(1)}, C_{\pi(2)}, \dots, C_{\pi(D)}]$$
